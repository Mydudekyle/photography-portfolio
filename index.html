About


I started out shooting behind the scenes and grew into directing, shooting and running social for private clients in Malibu over the last four years.

I like small crews and owning the whole process: planning the shoot, running camera, cutting the edit and getting it posted. Lately that's meant live streams over satellite internet, multicam podcasts, and building tools that turn long episodes into short clips.

I'm now building Kovenant Arc, a studio for cinematic brand and faith-based storytelling.
